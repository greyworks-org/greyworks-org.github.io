LullyTale

Baby sleep stories, lullabies, and soothing sounds.

Ages 0-4 · English & Turkish

What LullyTale does

LullyTale helps babies and toddlers drift into sleep with a library of carefully crafted sleep stories, gentle lullabies, and calming ambient sounds. Designed for parents who want a screen-off, audio-first bedtime routine.

The app includes a sleep timer that fades out automatically, a playlist builder for consistent routines, and bilingual content in English and Turkish.

01

Sleep stories

38+ original sleep stories and lullabies crafted to calm, not stimulate. Gentle pacing and soft language throughout.

02

Soothing sounds

White noise, rain, lullaby melodies, and ambient soundscapes. Mix and match to suit the room and the night.

03

Sleep timer

Set a timer and let the app fade out gently. Auto-stop keeps the routine hands-free once your child is asleep.
