Project

LullyTale

Baby sleep stories, lullabies, and soothing sounds.

Ages 0–4 · English & Turkish · Google Play

What LullyTale does

LullyTale helps babies and toddlers drift into sleep with a library of carefully crafted sleep stories, gentle lullabies, and calming ambient sounds. Designed for parents who want a screen-off, audio-first bedtime routine that actually works.

The app includes a sleep timer that fades out automatically, a playlist builder for consistent routines, and bilingual content in English and Turkish — with Spanish on the way.

Sleep Stories

38+ original sleep stories and lullabies crafted to calm, not stimulate. Each story uses gentle pacing and soft language to ease little ones toward sleep.

Soothing Sounds

White noise, rain, lullaby melodies, and ambient soundscapes. Mix and match to create the perfect sleep environment for your baby.

Sleep Timer

Set a timer and let the app fade out gently — no sudden stops to wake the baby. The auto-stop keeps the routine hands-free once your child is asleep.

Why parents trust LullyTale

  • Audio-first design — no screen time, just calming sound.
  • Bilingual library: English and Turkish content, with Spanish in development.
  • Ad-free experience with a premium subscription option.
  • Built with child safety and privacy as core requirements.
  • Regular content updates and seasonal stories.

Built by Greyworks

LullyTale is the first Greyworks-published app on Google Play. Every surface — from the store listing to the in-app audio player — was designed and built as part of Greyworks' digital product surface practice.

The project spans store presence, support flows, privacy policy, terms of use, and the app interface itself — a complete public-facing layer wrapped around a focused, useful product.
