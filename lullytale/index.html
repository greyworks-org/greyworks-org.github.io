LullyTale

Baby sleep stories, lullabies, and soothing sounds.

Ages 0-4 · English & Turkish · Google Play

What LullyTale does

LullyTale helps babies and toddlers drift into sleep with a library of carefully crafted sleep stories, gentle lullabies, and calming ambient sounds. Designed for parents who want a screen-off, audio-first bedtime routine.

The app includes a sleep timer that fades out automatically, a playlist builder for consistent routines, and bilingual content in English and Turkish.

Sleep Stories

38+ original sleep stories and lullabies crafted to calm, not stimulate. Gentle pacing and soft language.

Soothing Sounds

White noise, rain, lullaby melodies, and ambient soundscapes. Mix and match for the perfect sleep environment.

Sleep Timer

Set a timer and let the app fade out gently. Auto-stop keeps the routine hands-free once your child is asleep.

Story Library

Original tales crafted for bedtime calm

Inside the app

Audio-first, calm, and built for parents

Why parents trust LullyTale

Audio-first design: no screen time, just calming sound.
Bilingual library: English and Turkish, with Spanish in development.
Ad-free experience with a premium subscription option.
Built with child safety and privacy as core requirements.
Regular content updates and seasonal stories.
