About

We ship things that work.

Greyworks is not an agency. We are a founder-led collective that builds and ships real products, growth systems, and AI-powered operator stacks.

Who we are

Greyworks was founded by Utku Bozkurt, a strategy and business development leader with deep experience across SaaS, AdTech, and AI products. Previously CSO at WASK (B2B ad automation, 7K+ subscribers, 130+ countries) and founder of njoyKidz (500K+ downloads, $5M valuation).

We are not a team of 50. We are a small, focused collective that uses AI operator stacks to multiply output. One person with the right systems ships more than a department without them.

How we work

We build in the open, ship fast, and iterate based on real data. No pitch decks, no quarterly planning cycles, no committee approvals. If the product is solid and the market is there, we ship it.

  • Founder-led decisions, fast execution
  • AI-first workflow: agents, automation, intelligent systems
  • Real products over case studies
  • Growth systems baked into the product from day one

What we believe

Good products sell themselves when the surface is right, the positioning is clear, and the growth systems exist. Most products fail not because of the product, but because of everything around it.

That is where Greyworks operates: the layer between the product and the market. The store listing, the landing page, the growth loop, the operator stack that runs it all.
