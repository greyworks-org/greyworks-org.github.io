Account deletion requests

If you want Greyworks to delete your account and associated personal data for an app or service, email contact@greyworks.com from the email address linked to the account where possible.

Include the app or service name, the account email or identifier, and whether you are requesting account deletion, data deletion, or both. Greyworks may ask for reasonable verification before completing the request.

What happens next

  • Greyworks reviews the request and may ask for information needed to verify ownership of the account.
  • If the request is valid, Greyworks will delete or anonymise the relevant account data within a reasonable period.
  • Some limited records may be retained where reasonably necessary for fraud prevention, dispute handling, accounting, or legal compliance.

General support

For product support, bug reports, billing questions, or store-related issues, email contact@greyworks.com with the product name, device type, and a short description of the issue.

If the issue is about LullyTale, include the device model, OS version, and a short description of the issue.
