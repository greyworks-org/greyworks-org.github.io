Support

Support for Greyworks apps

This page is the public support contact for Greyworks websites, apps, and related digital products, including Painter Kid: Color Adventure on Google Play.

Last updated: 23 March 2026. Covers support requests for web, App Store, and Google Play products.

Email support

contact@greyworks.com

Typical help topics

Access issues, bug reports, ad reports, feature problems, and privacy-related requests.

Response details

Include the product name, device type, OS version, and a short description so the issue can be reviewed faster.

Painter Kid: Color Adventure

If your request is about Painter Kid: Color Adventure, include the app name, Android device model, Android version, and a short description of what happened.

  • For gameplay or stability problems, include screenshots if possible.
  • For ad-related reports, include the approximate time, what screen you were on, and a screenshot if available.
  • For parent or guardian questions about privacy or child safety, email contact@greyworks.com.

Before contacting support

  • Check that you are using the latest version of the app or product.
  • Include screenshots or error messages where relevant.
  • For purchase issues, mention the platform used for the purchase and the transaction date.

Account and privacy help

If your request relates to account access, data deletion, correction, or privacy rights, use the contact details on the contact page or email contact@greyworks.com.
