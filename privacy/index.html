Privacy Policy

How Greyworks handles personal information

This Privacy Policy explains how Greyworks collects, uses, stores, and shares personal information when people use the Greyworks website, iOS apps, Android apps, support channels, and related digital services.

Last updated: 23 March 2026. Applies to the website, App Store apps, Google Play apps, and related support or contact workflows.

Greyworks

Greyworks is an independent digital products business.

Contact

contact@greyworks.com

Scope

Website, apps, support requests, store submissions, and related services.

Painter Kid: Color Adventure and child-directed ads

Painter Kid: Color Adventure is a child-focused app. On Android, the app may use Google AdMob to show child-appropriate advertising in compliance with Google Play Families requirements.

  • Ads are intended to be non-personalized and configured for child-directed treatment.
  • Greyworks does not use a child’s name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, or similar personal content to target ads.
  • Google and related ad-service providers may process limited device, app, and contextual information needed to serve, limit, secure, and measure ads under their own policies.
  • If you have questions about ads shown in the app, contact contact@greyworks.com.

Information Greyworks may collect

Depending on how you use the services, Greyworks may collect:

  • Name, email address, account details, and contact information you provide.
  • Messages, support requests, forms, uploaded content, and account preferences.
  • Device, browser, operating system, app version, diagnostics, crash data, and usage information.
  • Transaction or billing details where a paid service, subscription, or purchase is involved.
  • Limited identifiers reasonably needed for login, fraud prevention, security, or service reliability.

How information is collected

Greyworks may collect information when you:

  • Create an account, download an app, submit a form, contact support, or communicate directly.
  • Use the website or apps, including through analytics, diagnostics, logs, and similar technical systems.
  • Grant app permissions such as notifications, camera, microphone, photos, files, or similar device access for a feature that needs them.
  • Complete a purchase through the App Store, Google Play, or another payment channel.

How Greyworks uses information

  • To provide, maintain, secure, and improve the website, apps, and support experience.
  • To operate accounts, deliver requested features, and respond to enquiries or support issues.
  • To process purchases, subscriptions, renewals, refunds, and required service communications.
  • To understand performance, usage patterns, reliability issues, and product improvement opportunities.
  • To detect abuse, prevent fraud, investigate incidents, and comply with legal obligations.

App permissions and device access

Some Greyworks apps may request access to device features such as notifications, camera, microphone, photos, media library, or files. Permissions are requested only when a feature needs them, and you can usually manage those permissions through your device settings.

If a permission is granted, the related data is used only to provide the requested feature, improve reliability, support the service, or meet legal and security obligations.

When information may be shared

Greyworks does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with:

  • Hosting, infrastructure, analytics, diagnostics, communications, and support providers.
  • Payment processors, App Store or Google Play services, and similar transaction partners.
  • Professional advisers, insurers, auditors, regulators, or law enforcement where required or reasonably necessary.
  • Service providers who process information on Greyworks' behalf for legitimate operational purposes.

Data retention and deletion

Greyworks keeps personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected, including support, security, accounting, legal, and dispute-resolution needs.

If you want to request deletion, correction, or access, contact contact@greyworks.com. Greyworks may keep limited records where retention is required for fraud prevention, legal compliance, payments, or legitimate business operations.

Your rights and choices

  • You may be able to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing depending on where you live.
  • You can usually control app permissions from your device settings.
  • You can opt out of non-essential communications using the unsubscribe method provided or by contacting Greyworks directly.

Children's privacy

Some Greyworks apps, including Painter Kid: Color Adventure, are designed for children or families. Where a child-focused experience requires adult involvement, a parent or guardian should review the relevant disclosures and supervise use.

If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact contact@greyworks.com and the request will be reviewed.

Security and international handling

Greyworks uses proportionate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information. No service can guarantee absolute security, but reasonable steps are taken to reduce the risk of unauthorised access, misuse, loss, or alteration.

Because service providers and tools may operate in more than one country, information may be processed outside your home country. Where this happens, Greyworks aims to use appropriate safeguards.

Changes to this policy

Greyworks may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When material changes are made, the date on this page will be updated and additional notice may be provided in the service where appropriate.
