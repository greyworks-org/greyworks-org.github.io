Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters.

How Greyworks handles personal information across websites, apps, support channels, and related services.

Last updated: 23 March 2026

Greyworks

Independent digital products business.

Contact

contact@greyworks.com

Scope

Website, apps, support requests, store submissions, and related services.

Information Greyworks may collect

Depending on how you use the services, Greyworks may collect:

  • Name, email address, account details, and contact information you provide.
  • Messages, support requests, forms, uploaded content, and account preferences.
  • Device, browser, operating system, app version, diagnostics, crash data, and usage information.
  • Transaction or billing details where a paid service or purchase is involved.
  • Limited identifiers needed for login, fraud prevention, security, or service reliability.

How information is collected

  • When you create an account, download an app, submit a form, or contact support.
  • Through analytics, diagnostics, logs, and similar technical systems.
  • When you grant app permissions for features that need them.
  • When you complete a purchase through a store or payment channel.

How Greyworks uses information

  • To provide, maintain, secure, and improve the website, apps, and support experience.
  • To operate accounts, deliver features, and respond to enquiries.
  • To process purchases, subscriptions, and service communications.
  • To understand performance, usage patterns, and improvement opportunities.
  • To detect abuse, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.

Analytics, advertising, and third-party services

Some services may use third-party tools for analytics, diagnostics, authentication, purchases, or advertising. Where advertising is shown, it may rely on limited device and contextual information. Where an app is intended for children, Greyworks aims to use age-appropriate settings.

When information may be shared

Greyworks does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with:

  • Hosting, infrastructure, analytics, and support providers.
  • Payment processors and store platform services.
  • Professional advisers, regulators, or law enforcement where required.

Data retention and deletion

Greyworks keeps personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary. To request deletion, correction, or access, contact contact@greyworks.com.

Your rights and choices

  • You may access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing.
  • You can control app permissions from your device settings.
  • You can opt out of non-essential communications.

Children's privacy

Some apps may be designed for children or families. A parent or guardian should review relevant disclosures and supervise use. Contact contact@greyworks.com if you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately.

Security

Greyworks uses proportionate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information. No service can guarantee absolute security, but reasonable steps are taken to reduce risk.

Changes to this policy

Greyworks may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When material changes are made, the date on this page will be updated.
